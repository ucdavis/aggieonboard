**DMV Employer Pull Notice**

Your position requires enrollment in the DMV Employer Pull Notice program in order for UC Davis Health to receive updates about your driving record.

You will receive a separate email from DocuSign with the form you need to complete. Please complete the form by {{DmvDeadline}}.

You will need to provide information from a California driver's license. Once you've submitted the form, no further action is needed unless we contact you for additional information.
<!-- end -->

<!-- if: Fingerprinting -->
**Fingerprinting**

Your position requires a **fingerprint background check**. Please complete the following steps:

**1. Schedule your appointment**

In your onboarding checklist, book with either the **UC Davis Health Police Department in Sacramento** or the **UC Davis Campus Police Department in Davis**.

Please select the earliest appointment that allows you to meet your pre-employment clearance deadline of **{{ClearanceDeadline}}**.

**2. Bring your authorization form and photo ID**
Print the attached **Background Check Authorization to Release Information** form and bring it with you, along with your **photo ID**, to your appointment.

**3. Enter your ATI number after your appointment**
After your fingerprints are taken, you will receive an Automated Transaction Identifier. The police department provides it as proof that you attended your appointment. Enter the ATI number in your onboarding checklist so we can track your Live Scan results.

Completing your appointment does not necessarily mean your Live Scan has cleared. We'll contact you if anything else is needed.
<!-- end -->

You will receive a separate email from DocuSign asking you to review and sign your position description.

You sign first; DocuSign then routes the document to your supervisor. After both signatures are complete, you can download a copy for your records.

Please complete your signature by {{PositionDescriptionDeadline}}.
