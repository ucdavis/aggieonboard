Welcome to the UC Davis Health onboarding process. I'm **{{OnboardingSpecialistName}}**, your onboarding specialist, and I'll help coordinate your pre-employment requirements.

Your anticipated start date is **{{StartDate}}**.

To keep that start date on track, all required clearances must be completed by **{{ClearanceDeadline}}**.

Please note that your start date remains tentative until your required pre-employment clearances are complete.

**Complete these steps now**

**1. Employee Health Screening**

Submit the **[OEHC appointment/request form]({{OehcFormUrl}}) immediately**:

Employee Health will call you within two business days to schedule your appointment in Sacramento, CA. You must attend the appointment at least two weeks before your start date.

**2. Drug screening — if required for your position**

You’ll receive an email from noreply@questdiagnostics.com with the subject “Your Drug Screen QPassport.” It includes your QPassport and collection-site instructions.

Complete your drug screen within 48 business hours of Quest sending the email. Check your inbox, spam and junk folders. If you don’t receive the email or cannot meet the deadline, contact me immediately.

**3. Background check**

If you have not already completed your background-check information, follow the emailed instructions you received from **Universal Background Screening**. Your background check must be cleared by {{ClearanceDeadline}} to maintain your anticipated start date.

**What happens next**

You’ll receive a separate email with instructions to complete your I-9 employment verification. Follow those instructions when they arrive.

Some requirements are handled by outside systems, so you'll receive separate emails containing the detailed instructions for those tasks.

Please complete each requirement promptly. Delays may affect your anticipated start date.

Questions about your overall clearance status or start date? Contact me at {{OnboardingSpecialistContact}}.
