**Complete before your first day**

Please complete the following items that are available to you now:

**1. Complete your new employee paperwork**
Within two hours, you will receive an email from DocuSign containing the forms that apply to your employment. Check your spam/junk/trash folders; contact your onboarding specialist if it has not arrived within 24 hours.

**2. Review the attached documents**

Review the attached documents and follow the instructions provided for each. Contact your onboarding specialist with questions.

**3. Claim your UC Davis Health account**

You’ll need to claim two accounts before you start:

- Health Active Directory account – for Health computers, email and network access.
- UC Davis Computing Account (Kerberos/CAS) – for UCPath and the UC Learning Center.

You should receive an email from the IT Service Desk with instructions. If you don’t receive it or need help claiming your accounts, visit the [account support page](https://computingaccounts.ucdavis.edu/cgi-bin/services/index.cgi) or call IT at 916-734-4357.

**4. Complete Privacy & Security Training**

After you claim your accounts, complete [Privacy & Security Training](https://health.ucdavis.edu/compliance/privacy/privacy-and-security-training) and email the final completion certificate to your onboarding specialist.

**On your first day**

Please:

- sign the [UC Davis Health Patent Acknowledgment](https://ucpath.universityofcalifornia.edu/personal-information/patent-acknowledgement)
- follow the arrival instructions provided by your department

**New Employee Welcome**

All new UC Davis Health employees are automatically assigned to a New Employee Welcome (NEW) session. You do not need to register.

Learning and Development will send your session details separately. NEW sessions are held twice a month at the Education Building on the UC Davis Health Sacramento campus, and attendance is required within your first 30 days of employment.

For questions, email hr-learning@ucdavis.edu with “Health NEW” in the subject line.
