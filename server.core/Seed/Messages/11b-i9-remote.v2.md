Hi {{FirstName}},

You've chosen remote I-9 verification.

In your onboarding checklist, enter the **full name** and **email address** of the adult who will serve as your authorized representative.

Your representative must be able to:

- meet with you in person;
- physically examine your original acceptable document(s); and
- access the online verification process using an internet-connected device.

Once we receive their information, an email with instructions to complete the Section 2 I-9 Verification will be sent from I9Tracker Complete.

The supplied remote template requires a representative over 18, with access to a PC, Mac, iOS or Android device and internet connectivity, who can read and understand Section 2. Once we receive the representative's contact information, I-9 Tracker Complete will email them instructions for completing Section 2. Remote verification still requires the representative to meet with you in person to examine your physical original documents.

[Official acceptable-document information](https://www.uscis.gov/i-9-central/form-i-9-acceptable-documents)
